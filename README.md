# Brainbaby free creative resources

English · [Türkçe](README.tr.md)

Plan an image edit or a presentation before opening an AI tool. This library contains **two fillable image-editing worksheets and 16 editable presentation outlines** from [Brainbaby](https://brainbaby.ai/tools#free-resources). Download the files without a Brainbaby account, adapt them to your project, and keep your own copy.

[**Download the complete library (ZIP)**](https://github.com/Brainbaby-OS/free-creative-resources/releases/download/v2026.10.05/brainbaby-free-creative-resources-2026-10-05.zip) · [Release details](https://github.com/Brainbaby-OS/free-creative-resources/releases/tag/v2026.10.05)

## Start with one resource

- **Editing a product photo?** Use the [English fillable PDF](resources/en/image-editing-checklist.pdf) or [editable Markdown worksheet](resources/en/image-editing-checklist.md). Record the change, the details to preserve and your review checks.
- **Preparing a presentation?** Choose an outline below. Replace its placeholders with your facts, then use the included AI brief in your preferred presentation workflow.

These are blank planning resources. The outlines are Markdown, not finished PowerPoint decks. The worksheets are not generated image samples or model-quality reports.

Each PDF has two pages and 24 fillable fields and checkboxes. Open it in a form-capable PDF app, fill it and save a local copy, or print it. To download a file from GitHub, open its link and choose **Download raw file**. A Markdown file can also be copied into a text editor.

For your own blue cup photo, an illustrative first edit brief is:

> Replace the cluttered background with a plain light-gray background. Keep the cup's shape, blue glaze and handle position unchanged. Do not add lettering or decorations.

After the edit, compare the full-size result with the original. Check the handle, edges, reflections, colors and any lettering. Record one correction at a time in the worksheet.

New to photo editing? Follow the [first AI photo-edit walkthrough](https://brainbaby.ai/blog/edit-photos-with-ai-for-free-step-by-step-guide): choose a source photo, write one change and preservation requirements, check the allowance, then compare the result. Its editable background example can be carried into Brainbaby's Image Studio. Preparing the brief and downloading these worksheets are free; image jobs have separate account and credit limits.

## A practical handoff

1. Save an untouched copy of your source material.
2. Fill the worksheet or outline with your audience, desired outcome and verified facts.
3. Give the brief and relevant source material to the tool or teammate doing the work.
4. Review the result. Keep missing information as placeholders rather than inventing figures, quotes or sources.
5. Save the source, brief, chosen output and remaining corrections together.

Brainbaby's online guides provide the matching workspace entry. Product accounts and image/presentation usage are separate from these free downloads; check [current plans and allowances](https://brainbaby.ai/pricing) before using the product.
