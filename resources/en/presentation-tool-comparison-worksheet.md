## 2. Use identical source notes in two shortlisted tools

Fictional retail delivery pilot:

- Four weeks at two stores.
- Delivery orders increased from 80 to 110 per week.
- Fulfillment cost increased from $4 to $6 per order.
- Customer feedback was positive, but the sample was small.
- Management must decide whether to continue, change the service area or stop.
- Revenue, margin, number of respondents and long-term effects were not provided.

Copy this task, followed by all the source notes above:

> Create a six-slide decision deck for a retail leadership team using only the supplied notes. Cover the decision, pilot scope, order volume, fulfillment cost, uncertainty and recommended next experiment. Do not invent revenue, profit, customer quotes or statistical significance. Identify missing information in speaker notes. Use one main point per slide and keep the final decision explicit.

## 3. Check the six slides

1. Decision: states the choice management must make, without inventing approval.
2. Scope: retains four weeks and two stores; does not generalize to all stores.
3. Orders: shows 80 and 110 per week. The increase is 30 or 37.5% relative to 80.
4. Cost: shows $4 and $6 per order. The increase is $2 or 50% relative to $4.
5. Uncertainty: identifies missing revenue, margin and feedback sample details. Profitability remains unknown.
6. Next experiment: labels the recommendation as proposed and explains what evidence is still needed.

A 37.5% increase in orders is not a 37.5% increase in revenue. Higher volume does not prove higher profit. Do not mix order counts and dollar amounts on one unlabeled chart axis. For a simple order-count bar chart, start the axis at zero. These calculations describe fictional input; they are not performance claims about Brainbaby or another tool.

## 6. Record the result in the blank CSV

https://brainbaby.ai/templates/en/presentation-tool-scorecard.csv

The CSV contains three empty attempt rows. Add one row for each actual tool and plan you test. It contains no formula totals, automatic recommendations or completed scores.

For each of the six criteria, enter:
- 0: does not meet the requirement.
- 1: needs meaningful correction.
- 2: meets the requirement after normal review.
- Blank: not tested. Record unavailable features in their separate column.

Criteria: evidence, story, editing, readability, handoff and fit within your budget. Do not let a high score override an essential requirement that failed. Keep currency amounts and credits separate. Record draft and correction time together. Do not extrapolate one attempt into a general speed or quality benchmark.
