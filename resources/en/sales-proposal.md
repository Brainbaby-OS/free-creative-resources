# Sales Proposal

A good sales proposal connects the buyer’s problem to a specific scope, price and decision. Build these eight slides from your discovery notes.

## 1. Buyer priorities

Restate the outcomes the buyer described. Quote discovery notes accurately and confirm the priorities.

## 2. Current situation

Summarize the existing process and measurable friction. Separate known facts from estimates.

## 3. Recommended solution

Describe the proposed approach and explain how each part addresses a buyer priority.

## 4. Scope and exclusions

List deliverables, owners and what is outside the price. Make dependencies explicit.

## 5. Implementation timeline

Show phases, milestones and customer responsibilities. Use realistic lead times.

## 6. Evidence

Include a relevant case study with permission, or a sample deliverable if no customer evidence exists.

## 7. Pricing

Show the total, payment schedule and any optional work separately. State the validity period.

## 8. Next steps

Name the decision owner, approval steps and a proposed kickoff date.
