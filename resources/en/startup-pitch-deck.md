# Startup Pitch Deck

Investors need a clear explanation of the business and evidence behind the opportunity. Use this 12-slide outline to prepare your story before designing the deck.

## 1. Problem

Describe a specific customer pain and the current workaround. Include an interview finding or observed cost, with its source.

## 2. Solution

Show the core workflow and the change it creates for the customer. Use a real product screenshot if available.

## 3. Customer

Define the first customer segment, buyer and user. Explain why this segment needs the solution now.

## 4. Market

Estimate the reachable market from customer count and expected spend. State your assumptions; avoid an unsupported headline market size.

## 5. Product

Walk through three steps in the product. Separate shipped capabilities from planned features.

## 6. Business model

Explain who pays, the price and the billing unit. Include gross margin assumptions and major costs.

## 7. Traction

Show dated revenue, retention or usage metrics. Label pilots and waitlist signups accurately.

## 8. Go-to-market

Describe one acquisition channel you have tested, its cost and the next experiment.

## 9. Competition

Compare alternatives on customer-relevant criteria, including doing nothing. Explain your specific advantage.

## 10. Team

Connect each founder’s experience to the problem. Identify important hiring gaps.

## 11. Financial plan

Show runway, spending and revenue assumptions. Include a conservative scenario rather than presenting a forecast as fact.

## 12. Investment ask

State the funding amount, use of funds and milestones it should unlock.
