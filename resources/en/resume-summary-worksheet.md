## 4. Review the opening

- [ ] The role and experience level are accurate.
- [ ] Every skill and achievement has a supporting fact above.
- [ ] Any number, date, title or qualification is supplied and accurate.
- [ ] Class projects, volunteering and paid work are described accurately.
- [ ] The wording fits this role without copying unearned requirements.
- [ ] Repeated phrases and generic adjectives are removed.
- [ ] I have checked the summary against the rest of the document.

These marks are your own review notes, not a hiring, ATS or document-quality score.
