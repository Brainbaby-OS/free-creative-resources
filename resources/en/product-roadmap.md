# Product Roadmap

A roadmap explains product direction without turning uncertain ideas into promises. Use a six-slide now, next and later structure.

## 1. Product objective

Define the customer outcome and business goal guiding the roadmap.

## 2. Customer evidence

Summarize research, usage data and support themes. Identify what still needs validation.

## 3. Now

Show committed work, owners and expected outcomes. Separate delivery dates from discovery tasks.

## 4. Next

Explain the next priorities and the dependencies that must be resolved first.

## 5. Later

Describe strategic themes rather than guaranteed release dates. Label uncertainty.

## 6. Tradeoffs and measurement

Explain what is deferred, why, and how you will evaluate success.
