# Investor Update

Keep investors informed with consistent metrics, candid progress and concrete requests. Use this eight-slide outline for a monthly or quarterly update.

## 1. Period summary

State the reporting dates and the most important change since the last update.

## 2. Key metrics

Use consistent definitions for revenue, retention and growth. Include previous-period comparisons.

## 3. Product progress

Separate shipped work from experiments and planned releases.

## 4. Customer learning

Summarize feedback and what changed because of it.

## 5. Financial position

Report cash, burn and runway with the assumptions behind the calculation.

## 6. Challenges

Explain material risks and what you are doing about them.

## 7. Next milestones

Name the next measurable outcomes and expected dates.

## 8. Investor asks

Request specific introductions, hiring help or advice with enough context to act.
