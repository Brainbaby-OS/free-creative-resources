## Drafting request

Prepare a draft for requirement IDs [IDs], using only the excerpts and approved evidence I supply. Return the matrix, proposed wording, missing evidence and review questions. Distinguish buyer requirements, proposed scope and verified supplier facts. Preserve qualifications, formats and limits. Leave unsupported answers TO CONFIRM. Do not invent a reference, certification, fee, delivery date or approval. Do not send or submit anything.
