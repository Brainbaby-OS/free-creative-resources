## Review checks
- [ ] Observation and expected source are separate from a cause hypothesis.
- [ ] Environment, repeat counts, logs and attachments are not invented.
- [ ] Proposed reproduction steps remain NEEDS RERUN until executed.
- [ ] Secrets and unnecessary personal information are removed.
- [ ] Duplicate search and final triage happen in the team tracker.
- [ ] No automatic ticket submission or successful fix is implied.
