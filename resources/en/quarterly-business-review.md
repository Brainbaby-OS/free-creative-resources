# Quarterly Business Review

A quarterly business review should explain performance, causes and decisions. Use this 10-slide outline to turn metrics into an accountable plan.

## 1. Executive summary

State the three most important outcomes and decisions needed.

## 2. Goals versus actuals

Compare the same reporting period, definitions and units. Mark missing data clearly.

## 3. Revenue and costs

Explain changes in revenue, spending and margin, with the data source.

## 4. Customer outcomes

Show retention, adoption or satisfaction trends alongside customer feedback.

## 5. Delivery performance

Compare committed work with completed work and explain scope changes.

## 6. Wins

Select two results and explain the actions that produced them.

## 7. Lessons

Describe a missed target, its likely cause and the evidence for that conclusion.

## 8. Risks

Assign an owner and mitigation to each material risk.

## 9. Next-quarter priorities

Choose a small number of measurable goals and name owners.

## 10. Decisions and actions

Record approvals needed, follow-up tasks and deadlines.
