# Case Study

Tell a customer story that can be checked. This six-slide structure connects the starting problem, the work and the measured result.

## 1. Customer context

Describe the customer and their starting situation with permission.

## 2. Challenge

Explain the goal, constraint and baseline metric.

## 3. Approach

Describe what was done and why, including customer responsibilities.

## 4. Implementation

Show the timeline and obstacles resolved. Separate product capabilities from manual work.

## 5. Results

Compare baseline and outcome over a stated period. Disclose other factors that may have contributed.

## 6. Lessons and next step

Include an approved quote, limitations and a relevant action for the reader.
